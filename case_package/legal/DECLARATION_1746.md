I, [DECLARANT NAME], declare as follows:

1. **Identity and authority.** I am [ROLE — e.g., the owner of the works at
   issue / an authorized agent of the owner / the [TITLE] of [ENTITY]], and I
   have personal knowledge of the facts stated here.

2. **Purpose.** This declaration authenticates the evidence submitted as
   Exhibits A through I and describes how it was collected and preserved.

3. **Methodology.** The evidence was collected and preserved using the
   procedures set out in the **Collection Methodology** attached as
   **Attachment 1** (`legal/METHODOLOGY.md`), which I have reviewed and which
   accurately describes the collection.

4. **What the exhibits are.** Exhibits A–I are the materials listed in the
   **Exhibit Index** (`legal/EXHIBIT_INDEX.md`), comprising: (A) attribution of
   the uploads; (B) the index of 12 infringing works and their URLs; (C) the raw
   backend data snapshots; (D) rendered page captures; (E) independent Wayback
   archival; (F) the site technology report; (G) RFC 3161 timestamp tokens;
   (H) the damages computation; and (I) the legal authority mapping.

5. **Electronic process (Rule 902(13)).** Exhibits A, B, C, F, H and I were
   generated by a defined electronic process and system — the scripts
   identified in the Methodology — that produces an accurate result. Each
   snapshot records its `source_url`, `row_count`, and `fetched_at`, and each
   is hashed.

6. **Hashed copies (Rule 902(14)).** Exhibits C, D, E and G include copies of
   data and files authenticated by a process of digital identification: the
   SHA-256 hash of each item is recorded in `evidence/manifest.json` and
   `captures/<run>/manifest.json`, and is reproduced in the Exhibit Index. I am
   informed and believe, and the computed hashes show, that the hashes of the
   submitted items are identical to those of the items as collected.

7. **Trusted timestamps.** Exhibit G contains RFC 3161 timestamp tokens,
   issued by a third-party Time Stamp Authority, over the SHA-256 digests of
   the evidence and capture manifests, establishing that those hashes existed
   no later than the times the tokens record.

8. **Public accessibility.** The works identified in Exhibit B were, at the
   time of collection, publicly accessible at the page URLs listed there and
   were served without any authentication beyond the site's own age gate. The
   media was served by the site's content delivery network to any request
   bearing the site's own referrer header.

9. **No alteration.** I have not altered the collected items; refreshes create
   new snapshots rather than modifying existing ones, and the version-control
   history preserves every prior snapshot.

10. **No unauthorized access.** The collection used only the target's publicly
    available interface and the anonymous key it publishes to every visitor. No
    authentication was bypassed, no privileged credential was used, and no data
    on the target was modified.

I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States of
America that the foregoing is true and correct.
